Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  30-day money-back guarantee on every order.

Our promise: if you are not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will make it right — a replacement or a full refund of the product price.

How to start a return

  • Email hello@kurryculum.com within 30 days of delivery.
  • Include your order number (from your receipt email) and the reason for the return.
  • We will reply with return instructions. Please do not send items back before contacting us.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days of approval. Your bank may take a few additional days to post the credit.
  • Original shipping charges, where applicable, are not refunded. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, email us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

Non-returnable items

  • Consultation bookings that have already taken place.
  • Products reported after the 30-day window has closed.

Order cancellations

If you need to cancel, email us as soon as possible. We can usually cancel an order before it ships. Once shipped, it falls under the return process above.

Shipping

  • Orders ship within 1–2 business days.
  • Free shipping on orders over 5.
  • You will receive tracking by email as soon as your order is on its way.

Need help?

We are a small, pharmacist-run brand and we want you to be happy. Email hello@kurryculum.com and we will take care of you.
