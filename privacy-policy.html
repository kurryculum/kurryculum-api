Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Kurryculum ("we", "us", "our") operates kurryculum.com.

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using kurryculum.com, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, shipping address, and phone number when you place an order, sign up for emails, take our quiz, or book a consultation.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see, collect, or store your card number.
  • Health and wellness information you choose to share: for example, goals or dietary preferences entered into our supplement quiz or nutrition calculator. This is used only to generate your recommendation.
  • Automatically collected data: IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, and referring site, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To process and ship your orders, and send order confirmations and receipts.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your questions.
  • To send marketing emails, if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To personalise supplement recommendations you request.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To detect fraud and keep the site secure.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and to understand how it is used. We also use Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing, which may use cookies to show you our ads on other sites after you visit ours. You can opt out of personalised Google advertising at adssettings.google.com, and you can block cookies in your browser settings.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Klaviyo — email delivery
  • Google — advertising and analytics
  • Webflow / Vercel — website hosting
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

We keep order records as long as needed for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. Marketing contacts are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the CCPA in California and the GDPR in Europe), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (note: we do not sell your data)

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@kurryculum.com and we will respond within the time required by law.

Children

Our site and products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Our supplements are intended for adults.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and a PCI-compliant payment processor. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.
